This privacy policy explains how Pocket Calculator ("the app"), published by AminZi Tech, Kabul, Afghanistan, handles information.

Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data. It has no internet permission, no advertising, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party SDKs.

Third parties

We share no data with any third party: no advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers or other companies. The app contains no third-party code that collects data.

Data stored on your device

Your calculation history, memory value and settings (language, sound, vibration) are saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage, so the app can restore them. This data never leaves your device and AminZi Tech cannot access it.

Sharing and copying

If you choose "Share" or tap the display to copy, the text is passed to the Android share menu or clipboard on your instruction. Where it goes next is decided by you and the app you pick.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. Vibration uses Android's built-in touch feedback and follows your phone's settings.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can clear history inside the app (History → Clear), or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

Security

Data is kept in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Children

The app is a general-audience utility. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same web address and inside the app with a new effective date.
